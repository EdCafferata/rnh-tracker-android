RNH Tracker is a free GPX tracker, originally built for the Ronde om Noord-Holland — handy for anyone who wants to record their route on the water or on the go.

🗺️ MULTIPLE MAP LAYERS
Choose OpenStreetMap, Carto DB, OpenTopoMap, or Carto DB Dark Matter, with optional OpenSeaMap sea marks layered on top.

📍 LIVE GPS TRACKING
Start/stop your track with one tap, see live distance and speed, and save your route as a standard GPX file.

📤 EXPORT AND SHARE
Every recorded track is saved as a .gpx file, ready to share or import into other navigation apps.

💶 FREE
No subscription, no paywall, no account needed.

Everything stays on your own device — there's no server and no tracking of you as a user.
